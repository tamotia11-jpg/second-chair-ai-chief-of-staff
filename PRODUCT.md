# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Leaders and operators at growing businesses evaluating how to introduce AI into the work they already run. They arrive looking for a credible, accountable partner rather than another self-serve tool.

## Product Purpose

Second Chair learns how a business operates, designs and connects useful AI capabilities, then remains responsible for operating, maintaining, and improving the deployment. The website must make this managed operating relationship clear and give a prospective customer a low-risk route into a focused paid pilot.

## Positioning

Second Chair is the managed operating layer between AI potential and reliable work: it combines a coordinated AI workforce, the customer's existing systems, control policies, and ongoing human accountability.

## Operating Context

The product is evaluated in the context of executive operations, sales, customer retention, marketing, support, and finance operations. A focused paid pilot is the first engagement; successful work can expand across the business only when evidence supports it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site is a Vite React application using owned shadcn/ui components and Lucide icons.
- Navigation, route behavior, the six-step pilot conversation, validation, privacy links, metadata, and accessibility affordances must remain functional.
- The site must not imply unrestricted automation: consequential work follows customer-defined control policies and appropriate human review.
- Do not invent customer testimonials, commercial benchmarks, or unsupported product claims.

## Brand Commitments

Second Chair is calm, precise, executive-facing, and candid. It should feel systems-minded and accountable, never like a speculative AI demo or an attention-seeking automation agency.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing source provides product copy, the operating-view illustration, capability descriptions, route content, security and privacy pages, and the pilot-conversation flow. No verified customer logos, testimonials, case studies, or outcome benchmarks are available for this redesign.

## Product Principles

- Start from the business as it runs today.
- Earn expansion through observed usefulness.
- Keep control proportional to risk and consequence.
- Remain accountable after deployment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion-safe interaction feedback.
