Start with a person,
not a platform.

Early adopters get a concierge implementation shaped around the way they already work. We map one recurring workflow, set approval rules, and run a specialist bench with you before anything is automated. Optional Manus-powered research and synthesis can sit behind the same approval rails.

  1. 01Pick the wedge

    Choose the recurring drag with frequency, urgency, and budget pressure.

  2. 02Set the rules

    Decide what can be drafted, what needs approval, and what stays private.

  3. 03Prove the signal

    Review outcomes weekly and keep only what genuinely replaces coordination labor.
