Website privacy notice.

This notice covers the public validation website at chair02.com. The hosted Second Chair product has a separate privacy policy linked from its native applications.

What the form collects

If you request a pilot outline, the form collects the workflow area, frequency, pain description, willingness-to-pay signal, name, work email, referral code, and submission time. Do not submit credentials, customer records, confidential company information, or private financial data.

Why it is used

The information is used only to evaluate pilot fit, reply to you, understand demand, prevent abuse, and improve this validation effort. It is not sold, used for ad targeting, or shared with ad networks.

Service provider and retention

The form is transmitted through FormSubmit and delivered to the project owner by email. Website code does not create a separate analytics profile or advertising identifier. Submissions are retained for up to 180 days unless they must be kept longer for security, legal, or dispute purposes.

Cookies and device storage

The public site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Browser storage is used only when running the form locally for development; public chair02.com submissions are sent to the email delivery service.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete a submission by emailing support@chair02.com from the same address used in the form. You may also contact that address with privacy questions.
